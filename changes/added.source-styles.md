**Pasted Documents Keep Their Layout**: `paste.blockStyles` keeps what Google Docs, Word or another editor sets on its blocks: paragraph spacing and line height, list markers, table and cell borders, backgrounds, padding, vertical alignment and column widths, Word's stylesheet spacing included. They show over the editor's own styles, so a pasted table looks as in its document. On by default in `prompt` mode, whose prompt gets a third choice: **Keep | Merge | Clean up**, Merge keeping the colors of the text while the blocks take the editor's layout, as Word's Merge Formatting does. Off in `merge` mode. HTML loaded with `setContent` keeps them too, and `getHTML()` gives them back.
